# Last-Song-of-Elysian-Music-add-on
Last Song of Elysian: Music Add-on introduces a portable radio and a collection of themed music discs. Players can record collected discs into the radio and enjoy their favorite music while exploring, building, or fighting.
The radio supports play, stop, previous track, next track, loop all, shuffle, repeat one, and volume controls, with storage for up to 64 tracks. Players can also import custom .ogg music files and assign PNG, JPG, or JPEG cover art to them. Its clean, vanilla-inspired interface blends naturally into Minecraft.

乐土终曲：音乐附加为游戏加入了可随身携带的音响与多张主题音乐唱片。玩家可以将收集到的唱片录入音响，在探索、建造和战斗时随时播放喜爱的音乐。
音响支持播放、停止、上一首、下一首、列表循环、随机播放、单曲循环以及音量调节，最多可保存 64 首曲目。此外，玩家还可以导入自己的 .ogg 音乐文件，并为自定义曲目设置 PNG、JPG 或 JPEG 格式的封面。操作界面采用 Minecraft 原版风格，简洁直观，并能自然融入游戏。
